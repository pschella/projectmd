# Weight watch: LM mass recovery, bottom-up list

- Owner: Kofi Mensah <kofi.mensah@lm-co.example.net>
- Commitment: 1965-04-30 .. 1966-07-22
- Done: 1966-07-29

My working list of mass savings in the lunar module, one concrete change per line. Savings are
rough shop estimates in kg; durations are my own estimates of engineering work. The Commitment
above is the promise to the LM programme: the mass forecast for the flight article, radar included.
The list is the structures team's mass programme; LM links it with a firm need-by of 1966-07-26. The programme closed
1966-07-29, seven days after its Commitment, with a total saving of 19 kg (about 11 kg with done dates, 7 kg done as planned, and a
0.8 kg radar bracket).
