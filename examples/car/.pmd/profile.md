## State vocabulary

- `concept` — idea approved for development; no design freeze yet
- `discovery` — requirements still open; dates are coarse
- `planned` — scope agreed; work not started
- `sourcing` — supplier being selected or nominated
- `qualification` — supplier, process or part approval in progress
- `in-development` — active work toward the commitment

## Extra known key

- `ticket:` — external tracker id, free text (for example `ticket: PACK-311`)
